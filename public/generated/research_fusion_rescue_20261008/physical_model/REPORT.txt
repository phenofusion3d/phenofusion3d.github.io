PHYSICAL SCAN-CAMERA RESCUE: 8 OCTOBER 2026

Scope
-----
These are new camera-model experiments. Original calibration, reconstructions,
spectra, earlier results and hardware/software capture paths were not changed.
No spectra have been assigned or interpolated by these experiments. Both FX10
and FX17 were tested; additional independently reviewed training landmarks were
available only for FX10. None of the new reserved landmark coordinates or scores
were consumed while fitting or selecting the frozen models.

Coordinate audit
----------------
The old metric conversion multiplies old reference XYZ exactly once by
1.0288746669066682. Board XYZ and bridge translation are already metric. The row-
vector inverse bridge is (P_plant - t) @ R, which is correct. The metric board
basis and bridge rotation are orthonormal within floating-point precision.
There is no demonstrated scale, inversion, normal-sign or axis-swap bug here.
The camera-to-plant bridge is small but provisional; it is separately tested
below using the original recorded paper matches.

Physical model
--------------
For a fixed-orientation line camera moving as C(t)=C0+v*t, the sensor-line plane
condition is e_y dot (P-C(t))=0. Thus scan time/line is affine in XYZ. At that
time, the detector column is a ratio of two affine functions. Translating P in
the known gantry direction changes scan line but leaves detector column fixed.

We therefore use X parallel to the measured FULL 3D gantry direction, with Y,Z
transverse, and fit:

  column = (ay*Y + ah*Z + a0) / (1 + by*Y + bh*Z)
  line   = lx*X + ly*Y + lh*Z + l0

This removes spurious along-motion detector-column terms allowed by the older
unconstrained planar rational fit. All board coordinates and exposed elevated
controls are fitted jointly. Bounds retain a positive denominator at the actual
leaves and avoid implausibly near-parallel scan-plane configurations. Optical
centre priors are broad hypotheses, not manufacturer-calibrated intrinsics.
Reported focal/height interpretations assume approximately orthogonal scanning;
they are diagnostic interpretations, not an independently recovered full rig.

Identifiability
---------------
With truly planar Z=0 observations, three of nine coefficients (ah,bh,lh) are
unobservable. The nominal board surfaces depart from their mean plane by only
about +/-0.35 mm, including pose and paper uncertainty, whereas these plant
features are about 350-392 mm above it. That tiny apparent non-planarity cannot
be treated as accurate elevated control evidence. Image-plane fits can therefore
be excellent while many different elevated projections remain plausible.

Exposed-control experiments
--------------------------
Four camera prior configurations were fitted per sensor, preserving all results.
Each includes leave-one-old-feature and whole-board-sheet removal. These are
selection diagnostics: the three old P5 correspondences and old sheet controls
were already inspected in earlier work. They are not fresh final validation.

Unregularized translating-camera joint fits have table RMS 0.633 px FX10 and
0.337 px FX17, but exposed elevated RMS 6.392 and 5.670 px respectively. The
stronger near-nadir prior reduces the worst FX10 leave-one-old-feature error from
129.94 to 35.84 px. For FX17 the weak optical prior gives the best worst error,
12.24 px, compared with 89.44 px without it. No tested prior makes these three
uncertain observed centres mutually precise while retaining the table fit.

The red feature pair has native line separation 53.578 px FX10 / 52.762 px FX17,
but the corresponding table-calibrated geometry predicts approximately 44.689 /
44.773 px. The 8.889 / 7.989 px discrepancies cannot be explained by the tiny
0.241 mm difference in their motion-frame heights using a sensible constant
scan-plane tilt. If every other coefficient is held fixed, that would require
about 88 degrees. This is a conflict of the current observations with that
specific model; it does NOT identify camera height as the sole error source.

Independent visual audit judged the same red neighbourhoods plausible, but the
automatic feature centres are not surveyed physical points. Approximate HSI
localization uncertainty is 3-4 px and native RGB uncertainty 2-3 px, subjectively
assessed. Thus the former 3-pixel screen is not physical ground truth and should
not be applied as though these centres were exact.

Alternative explanations actually tested
---------------------------------------
1. Scan-speed/transfer drift: linear and quadratic fits to previously exposed
   static 002-to-003 controls predict only 0.003-0.011 px differential line shift
   over this red pair. Smooth drift supported by those controls cannot explain
   the approximately 8-9 px discrepancy. Previously exposed static controls
   and leave-one-feature errors are retained.
2. RGB run bridge: two scan cameras and the six-DOF bridge were jointly optimized
   against the original 386 unique recorded paper correspondences, board points
   and old elevated correspondences. HSI uncertainty was relaxed to 4 px.
   RGB support sigma 1,2,5 px was tested; 5 px is explicitly a stress relaxation.
   Full fits retain paper RMS about 1.27 px but elevated RMS about 6.38-6.44 FX10
   and 5.65-5.71 FX17. The joint bridge does not remove the discrepancy.
3. Newly audited material features: two FX10 features were added BEFORE any
   reserved-point scores were available. Three optical priors were tested with
   direct depth-lift XYZ and with nearest original cloud XYZ: six frozen models.
   Primary: weak optical prior + direct depth lift, avoiding a nearest-vertex
   shift. Board RMS 0.632 px; five-feature training RMS 5.651 px.
4. Rendered-geometry colour/silhouette search: 18,215 existing red/green P5 points
   were projected into the saved native FX10 pseudoRGB image. Three height/ray
   coefficients were optimized against same-colour-region distance, not reserved
   point labels. Three starting values were retained. A first implementation
   wrongly penalized genuinely out-of-FOV geometry and favoured shrunken leaves;
   that failed variant remains saved. Version 2 censors out-of-FOV points and
   applies an explicit minimum-visibility sensitivity penalty.

The final colour-objective minimum has 90.85% of projected green-class points
and 98.41% of red-class points within 3 native pixels of the same observed colour
class. This is broad region agreement, NOT a 3-pixel material-point error, a
visibility proof, or a dense fusion accuracy score. It has a near-orthogonal
camera-height interpretation of 1.028 m and principal column 527.65 px.

The two distinct primary models disagree by median 5.52 px and 95th percentile
13.47 px over 145,459 existing P5 points that project inside FX10 in both models.
This demonstrates why a convincing leaf silhouette still needs independent
material-point checks. This spread is model disagreement, not a calibrated
uncertainty interval. The colour-class objective does not explicitly recover
per-point occlusions; it cannot certify which overlapping surface was observed.

Frozen-model independent checks
-------------------------------
The control-audit agent received frozen_candidates.json and
frozen_silhouette_candidates_v2.json with the complete parameters, bridge,
coordinate basis, pixel translation and evaluation recipe. It withheld five
new material-point labels and their scores during all fitting. Scores are saved
separately in control_audit, with subjective localization radii and sensitivity
to direct depth-lift versus nearest original vertex. These remain image-based
checks on this recording, not independently surveyed physical targets.

After freezing, the agent released the following native FX10 HSI residuals:

  Reserved feature           Physical primary     Colour-objective primary
  Red right notch                 4.865 px               5.354 px
  Red distal tip                  8.842 px               6.520 px
  Green basal notch               8.833 px               8.618 px
  Green basal vein (LOW identity) 26.839 px              35.749 px
  Green distal vein               5.945 px              19.845 px

The last green vein's prediction also moves by 5.60 px when direct depth lift
is replaced with the nearest original vertex, illustrating source localization
sensitivity. The low-identity green basal vein was labelled low before scoring;
it is retained but must not alone decide acceptance. No landmark is eligible for
a strict physical-accuracy gate: their subjective uncertainty is about 4-5 HSI
pixels and 3 RGB pixels, plus 3D localization/model uncertainty. No calibrated
confidence intervals are available.

The independently reserved checks support a useful coarse projection but do not
establish precise dense material-point mapping. Optimizing broad colour overlap
does not reliably improve the checks, particularly on the green distal vein.
No fit was changed after these scores were released. The saved outputs are model
experiments, not a newly validated dense spectral fusion product. A limited
approximate overlay, if shown separately, must state these measured image-space
residuals and the limits of the control identities, rather than calling the
registration perfect or the manually assigned control centres exact.
