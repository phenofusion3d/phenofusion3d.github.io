What the saved records show

  • The September 28 RGB-D recordings retain camera intrinsics but no saved depth-unit value. Legacy source reads the value without writing it.
  • Filenames use a source convention of ROS position multiplied by one million. The executed capture version and calibrated driver units are not independently recorded.
  • The September HSI folder has four cubes and four headers, with no per-line sidecars. Historical August 28 sidecars retain frame IDs and timing fields; these identify exactly which originals to recover for September.
  • Historical board designs specify 600 pixels, not physical print dimensions. Historical homographies and specimen ICP transforms are not camera spatial calibration.

The historical metadata was not transferred to current runs. Unknown millimetre scale is separate from the possibility of pixel-validated mapping in a consistent arbitrary frame.
