Reproduction and limits

The saved driver contains the exact environment settings, full selection and repeat invocation; complete machine-readable results record commands, timings, imports, and snapshot comparisons. It expects a fresh source_v2 destination and deliberately refuses to overwrite this evidence. Any future rerun should use a new destination.

The 20 repeats invoke the exact changed test with mocked ROS startup; they support the correction to the observed test race, without proving that every possible scheduling condition is eliminated. The optional real-recording parity case, physical lab acceptance, and scientifically calibrated measurements remain separate checks. This temporary dependency environment is evidence of offline compatibility, not a packaged or supported production runtime.
