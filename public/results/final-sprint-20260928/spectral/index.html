How to read values and gaps

Raw DN is the recorded camera signal. Q uses an assumed dark correction; Q0 uses zero offset. Both are reference-relative sensitivity results. Unknown white-board reflectance and an unconfirmed dark reference prevent calibrated reflectance or plant-health claims.

At the three 3D locations, both red-leaf spectra lack valid reference support, so their Q/Q0 and descriptors remain missing. The green-leaf point has finite Q/Q0 in both sensors. FX10 can calculate its six saved descriptors there; FX17 supplies one separately derived signal ratio. No missing value is filled or interpolated.

Correspondences are provisional source-feature matches, including a composed FX17-to-FX10-to-RGB/3D association. They are not independently validated physical calibration. The board dimensions are now confirmed: 25 mm cells and 18 mm markers. The D405 geometry has been expressed in that metric scale. A shared table-plane calibration and static scan transfer are saved; the three-control height model failed its untouched-point tests, so dense spectral projection is not accepted.
