FUSION AUDIT AND NEW LOCAL REGISTRATION EXPERIMENTS
8 October 2026

This report distinguishes the saved confirmed-board v1 checkpoint from the new
experiments in ../fusion_candidates. Additional work by the main task may produce
a later candidate export. The three-marker result is not the requested completed
dense five-plant spectral surface model.

WHAT THE THREE MARKERS MEAN

The v1 inspector shows three original reconstructed Plant 5 locations. Each has
one FX10 observation and one separately recorded FX17 observation: six spectra at
three unique locations, each sensor retaining its own 224 bands. A wavelength
change selects a different measured value at those locations and changes their
display colour. The other 430,057 of the 430,060 context points have no assigned
spectral observation. They cannot be described as a spectrally fused whole plant.

13,343 sampled camera-space spectra exist (7,982 FX10 and 5,361 FX17), but a
measured spectrum's existence does not determine which reconstructed material
point it belongs to. Image-space analysis and 3D material-point association are
separate deliverables. Camera data identify FX10, not FX15.

WHY THE OLDER PICTURE IS DENSER

The experimental website reports a different three-plant acquisition from
28 August. Reading the website's actual saved assets gives 5,560 Coleus, 2,539
fuzzy kalanchoe and 2,386 succulent/jade projected spectral points: 10,485 total.
The later local replay gives different counts, so it must not substitute for the
published historical result when describing that image.

Its method is an image-registration prototype:
1. White/dark ROI normalization and spatial stride 4; FX17 gain/offset adjustment.
2. Resize and affine-warp FX17 onto FX10 in image space. The merged 427-band
   product uses FX10 <=1000 nm and registered FX17 >1000 nm. FX17 values in this
   merged product have been spatially resampled; it is not an untouched 427-band
   detector observation at one native pixel.
3. Register FX10 image features to a selected RGB view using SIFT and RANSAC.
   Coleus uses a plant-local homography with 16 training inliers and 4.028 px RMS;
   fuzzy kalanchoe uses 8 with 1.824 px RMS. These are fit residuals.
   Jade's saved model says board plus ECC, but ECC correlation is null and its
   residual matrix is identity: no successful nonidentity ECC refinement is
   demonstrated by that saved record.
4. Round mapped RGB pixels, search up to four pixels for valid depth, backproject
   with RGB-D intrinsics, remove duplicated depth pixels and background layers,
   and retain points within 20 mm of the existing cleaned specimen surface.
5. Recover a per-plant rigid transform against the legacy ICP scene and display
   the spectral points on the grey geometry. The legacy acquisition had omitted
   the full saved pose trajectory, so this was dataset-specific pose recovery.

Those coloured dots carry processed measured spectra. Their assignment to a
particular leaf location is an approximate image warp, not independently
calibrated pushbroom material-point truth. Zero distance to a specimen surface
built from the same RGB-D view is expected for overlapping source pixels and
does not independently validate the hyperspectral match. This limitation does
not invalidate the prototype, but its visual density is not proof of accuracy.

HOW D405 AND L515 WERE FUSED IN THE NEW STUDY

Both geometries were placed into a metric reference using the confirmed 25 mm
board and an empirical rigid alignment. The union preserved the selected
1,719,001 D405 points and added 21,672 L515 points. Near duplicates were suppressed,
cross-view free-space contradictions rejected, and uncertain overlaps excluded.
Of the additions, 14,220 have at least two agreeing D405 stereo views and 7,452
are complementary L515-only candidates. Positions were not averaged into new
surfaces and missing leaf shapes were not invented. The 1,740,673-point union was
then separated into plant, uncertain and scene-context selections; the retained
plant subset has 430,060 points. This geometry fusion is separate from attaching
hyperspectral observations.

WHAT THE BOARD FIXED, AND WHAT IT DID NOT

The recorded calibration is used: 205 sheet-qualified HSI controls share the
metric D405 table frame. Whole-sheet affine holdouts are 0.905-1.385 px RMS for
FX10 and 0.475-0.679 px for FX17. Static calibration-to-plant scan transfer is also
supported by recorded features. These controls lie essentially on one table
plane; they do not uniquely determine how rays meet raised leaves.

A bounded height-extension test fitted two of the three P5 points and predicted
the third. It failed: FX10 errors 10.08, 14.48, 174.70 px; FX17 7.24, 12.28,
123.43 px. That specific model was properly rejected. It does not prove that all
local leaf methods must fail.

NEW WORK ON 8 OCTOBER: LOCAL LEAF METHODS

A new feature sweep tested affine and homography candidates on two recognizable
P5 leaves in RGB frame 1055904. Reviewed point anchors and all nearby descriptors
were excluded from fitting. Across eight fits, no model met the declared screen.
The best red affine predicted the two excluded anchors with 16.11 and 37.08 px
errors; the best green affine missed its excluded anchor by 8.02 px. Training
RANSAC inliers alone would have concealed these problems. Results and annotated
panels are in ../fusion_candidates/local_warp_probe.json.

The FX10-to-FX17 leg is more promising. A new lower-green-leaf fit used nearby
measured wavelengths around 945, 970, 990 and 1001 nm with exact pixel-centre
conversion. Of 66 repeatable matches outside the excluded anchor neighborhood,
54 support a local affine model. It predicts the existing green FX17 anchor with
0.589 px error. Leave-out source-column thirds have median errors 0.699, 0.914,
1.029 px, but the untrimmed first group's RMS is 21.01 px because one gross
false/background match has 98.26 px error. This false match is preserved, not
silently discarded to advertise a small RMS. Other two groups' RMS are 1.18 and
1.22 px. These are image consistency checks, not physical calibration.

Pixelwise forward/backward tracking was then tested across all four overlap
bands. 11,023 of 37,570 queried native FX10 pixels pass the declared tracking
gates, corresponding to 6,984 unique rounded FX17 pixels. This is a registration
support mask, not a plant mask or a dense 3D export. It must be intersected with
reviewed same-leaf masks, an independently checked FX10-to-RGB domain, supported
depth/pose and exact original reconstructed point identities. The main task is
testing that missing HSI-to-RGB leg separately. No spectra or prior results were
changed by these tests.

DATA STILL TO RECOVER IF LOCAL MAPPING IS INSUFFICIENT

All current paired calibration 002 and plant 003 HSI cubes are accounted for.
Their headers have dimensions, wavelengths/FWHM and a run timestamp, but no
spatial lens/ray calibration, rig extrinsics or per-line motion/timing. No omitted
current HSI cube was found. Recovering those applicable rig records, or recording
measured elevated controls across plant heights with reserved holdouts, would
support a stronger physical model. The board dimension is now resolved and
should not be repeated as a present blocker.

White-panel reflectance and dark-reference confirmation remain separate gaps.
They limit calibrated reflectance, not the ability to demonstrate geometry with
raw measured DN. Local candidate maps should retain both sensors separately,
missing values explicitly, and original integer spectral pixel identities.
