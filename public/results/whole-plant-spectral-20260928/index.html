Raw values from different cameras are not directly comparable.

What does a point on the graph mean?

One camera pixel records 224 values. Each graph point is its signal at one wavelength. The curve shows how that small visible area responds across the camera’s wavelength range.

What changed from the islands?

The old view exported small selected patches. Here, no spatial patch selection is required: the complete scan stays visible and every source pixel is available, including background.

What can we claim?

Measured spectral signals across five visible plant regions, with optional RGB context. The white board’s reflectance is unknown and the dark tail is assumed, so neither raw DN nor Q is validated reflectance or a plant-health diagnosis.
